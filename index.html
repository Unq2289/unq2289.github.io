01 — WHO WE ARE

ABOUT NOVA

Where ideas become experiments, and experiments become innovation.

01
⚡

OUR VISION

NOVA envisions a student-driven ecosystem at NIT Delhi where curiosity turns into experimentation, experimentation turns into projects, and projects create real-world impact. We aim to empower students to explore technology, challenge conventional thinking, and build solutions that go beyond the classroom.

02
◈

OUR MISSION

NOVA brings students together to participate in hackathons, technical competitions, workshops and collaborative projects. From college-level hackathons to national platforms like the Smart India Hackathon, we provide students with opportunities to apply what they learn, work in teams, solve real problems and continuously improve through hands-on experience.

03
⌁

WHAT WE DO

NOVA takes ideas from competitions and turns them into continuous projects. We participate in SIH and other hackathons, conduct college-level technical events, and develop projects that continue evolving beyond competitions. From brainstorming an idea to building, testing and improving it, NOVA focuses on creating a culture of continuous innovation and hands-on engineering.

04
∞

OUR COMMUNITY

NOVA is a student-led community where beginners can start exploring, experienced members can share their knowledge, and everyone gets the opportunity to contribute. We believe the best learning happens when people build together—sharing ideas, solving problems, making mistakes and growing through the process.

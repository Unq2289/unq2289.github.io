## ⚠️ Why Do You Need This Tool?

Over time, your computer accumulates broken shortcuts. This happens when:

- You uninstall a program but its shortcut remains
- You move or rename a file or folder that a shortcut points to
- An external drive or USB stick is removed
- Software updates change file locations
- Old programs leave behind leftover icons

These dead shortcuts waste space, make your desktop look messy, and can slow down your system when it tries to load them. Cleaning them regularly keeps your computer fast and organized.
